Kawan: an open assistive robot

For non-verbal autistic students in Singapore SPED schools: coaching daily-living and job skills step by step, noticing when a learner needs more support, and staying calm and safe during a meltdown.

The plan

A · Workstation coach

A tabletop robot at a structured work desk. It shows each step, checks the work tray with a camera, and adjusts its prompts. Comes first.

B · Learning lab

A low-cost open-source robot with arms that learns table-setting with chopsticks and tidying by demonstration. Lab only, never with students.

C · Full-size robot

Mobile base, lift mast, two arms and a guide handle. Cooks through appliances, and guides people to stairs but never carries them.

Firm rules

  • The robot never touches, blocks or restrains a person.
  • Everything runs offline on the robot. No feature depends on a cloud service staying in business.
  • No student data on this site or in the repository.
  • Nobody controls a physical robot through this site.
